# Freshroots Malawi — Content and Asset Requirements

Use this checklist to collect client-approved content. Do not fill gaps with invented information.

## Brand assets
- [ ] Original logo in SVG or vector PDF.
- [ ] Transparent PNG logo.
- [ ] Approved logo variants and favicon.
- [ ] Any existing brand guidelines.
- [ ] Confirm approved tagline (optional).

## Company information
- [ ] Approved company name.
- [ ] Company introduction and history.
- [ ] Mission, vision and values (if available).
- [ ] Public-facing location wording.
- [ ] Confirm whether Mzuzu and Nkhata Bay describe office, farm, service or social profile location.

## Farming operations
- [ ] Rice production details.
- [ ] Maize production details.
- [ ] Crop varieties (if confirmed).
- [ ] Verified harvest/supply periods.
- [ ] Verified production capacity and acreage, if approved for publication.
- [ ] Processing, storage, packaging and logistics details, if these operations exist.
- [ ] Quality standards and certifications, only with evidence.

## Agro-Store
- [ ] Confirmed opening status/date.
- [ ] Confirmed product categories.
- [ ] Product names and descriptions.
- [ ] Product photographs.
- [ ] Product specifications and safety information.
- [ ] Prices and availability, if to be published.
- [ ] Enquiry and fulfilment process.

## Corporate supply
- [ ] Target buyer categories.
- [ ] Bulk order process.
- [ ] Minimum order quantities, if any.
- [ ] Delivery areas and terms.
- [ ] Procurement contact person or business email.
- [ ] Approved company profile PDF.

## Contact and social
- [ ] Business telephone number.
- [ ] Business email.
- [ ] Physical/postal address, if public.
- [ ] WhatsApp number, if approved.
- [ ] Instagram and Facebook URLs.
- [ ] Other official social channels.

## Photography
- [ ] Wide rice-field hero photograph.
- [ ] Maize field photograph.
- [ ] Crop detail photographs.
- [ ] Farming activity/team photographs (with consent).
- [ ] Harvest photographs.
- [ ] Storage/packaging photographs, if applicable.
- [ ] Agro-store/products photographs when available.

## Publishing check
- [ ] Confirm all claims and figures with client.
- [ ] Obtain permission for staff/customer photographs.
- [ ] Confirm rights to all images and logos.
- [ ] Remove development placeholders before launch.
- [ ] Test every published contact and enquiry pathway.
