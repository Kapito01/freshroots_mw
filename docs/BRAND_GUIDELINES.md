# Freshroots Malawi — Brand Guidelines

## Brand foundation
Freshroots is an integrated agribusiness in Malawi, currently focused mainly on rice and also maize, with plans to open an agricultural supplies store.

**Positioning:** A modern African agricultural company: credible, grounded, progressive and commercially capable.

**Brand personality:** Trustworthy, practical, contemporary, accessible and connected to Malawi.

**Working tagline (not client-approved):** “Growing with purpose. Rooted in progress.” Keep this as a draft until the client confirms it.

## Existing identity to preserve
The client-provided Instagram profile screenshot shows:
- A circular emblem containing a stylised “F”.
- Olive/leaf-green lettering and emblem.
- Warm cream/off-white background.
- “FRESHROOTS MALAWI” arranged around/under the emblem.

The screenshot is a reference, not a production-quality logo asset. Request the original SVG, vector PDF or transparent PNG before creating final logo lockups. Do not redraw the emblem inaccurately or replace it without approval.

## Proposed digital palette
These are working web colour values inspired by the visible social identity. They have not been sampled from the original logo file and require final client approval.

| Token | Hex | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Freshroots Olive | #687C4C | Brand accent, links, small highlights |
| Harvest Cream | #F6F1DF | Warm page backgrounds |
| Deep Forest | #253C2D | Primary corporate emphasis, dark sections |
| Grain Gold | #B69B68 | Restrained accents only |
| Earth Charcoal | #292C27 | Main text |
| Pure White | #FFFFFF | Content surfaces |

Use warm white/cream as the dominant surface, charcoal for body text and forest/olive green for emphasis. Do not make every section green. Gold should be rare.

## Typography
Working recommendation:
- Headings: Manrope (or a suitable system fallback).
- Body and UI: Inter (or a suitable system fallback).
- Use a clear hierarchy, restrained weights and comfortable line lengths.
- Avoid overly decorative or rustic fonts.

## Photography
Prioritise authentic Freshroots photographs:
- Rice fields and rice crop details.
- Maize fields and harvest.
- Farming activities and people, with permission.
- Storage, handling, packaging and distribution, if these operations exist.
- Agricultural supplies store and products when available.

Use large landscape compositions and natural light with restrained colour grading. Never imply generic or generated imagery depicts Freshroots' actual farm, staff, facilities or stock. Mark placeholders clearly in development.

## Voice and messaging
Write in concise, clear and commercially credible language. Sound confident but never boastful. Explain what Freshroots actually does. Avoid unsupported superlatives and claims.

## Asset rules
Keep approved logo variants under `public/brand/` and approved photography under organised folders in `public/images/`. Preserve original files. Use descriptive filenames and meaningful alt text.
