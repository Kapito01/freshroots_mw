# Freshroots Malawi — Website Requirements

## Business context
Freshroots describes itself as an integrated agribusiness focused on producing quality crops. Current main crops are rice and maize. The business plans to open a store for agricultural protective gear, fertilizers and related supplies.

Primary audience:
- Corporate procurement teams.
- Food processors and manufacturers.
- Wholesalers and distributors.
- Supermarkets, retailers and institutions.
- Business partners.

Secondary audience:
- Farmers and general customers interested in agricultural products.

## Primary website goals
1. Establish a credible corporate digital presence.
2. Explain the business and its operations clearly.
3. Present rice and maize production accurately.
4. Create a clear path for bulk supply and partnership enquiries.
5. Introduce the upcoming Agro-Store and its product categories.
6. Make it easy to contact Freshroots.

## Initial release pages
### Home
Summarise Freshroots, show its agricultural focus and direct visitors to operations, Agro-Store and corporate enquiries.

### About
Explain the company, its purpose and values using client-approved information only.

### Operations
Present rice and maize separately. Include production details only when verified by the client.

### Agro-Store
Initial product catalogue/category structure for fertilizers, crop protection, protective clothing, safety equipment, tools and other supplies. Show only confirmed products. Initial scope is enquiry-led; no checkout or payment flow.

### Corporate Supply
Explain the bulk enquiry process and provide a structured request-for-quotation form if a verified recipient/backend is configured.

### Contact
Show client-approved phone, email, physical address, social links and enquiry options. Unknown details must remain placeholders in development and must not be published as fabricated facts.

## Initial release features
- Responsive corporate website.
- Reusable content and product presentation components.
- Product category/catalogue presentation.
- Corporate supply enquiry form.
- Contact form or direct contact pathways, once destination is configured.
- WhatsApp link only after the client confirms the business number.
- Downloadable company profile only after the profile is supplied and approved.
- Basic technical SEO, page metadata and social sharing metadata.
- Accessible navigation and page structure.

## Future phase features
- Full e-commerce cart and checkout.
- Online payment integrations.
- Live stock and inventory management.
- Customer accounts.
- Product traceability.
- Seasonal agriculture calendar.
- Farm/operations map with client-approved locations.
- Internal product and enquiry management dashboard.

## Form requirements
- Use clear labels and helpful validation messages.
- Collect only information necessary to respond.
- Provide confirmation and error states.
- Do not imply that an enquiry has been sent unless a real backend/email integration succeeds.
- Protect endpoints from spam and validate input server-side.
- Do not commit credentials.

## Content and approvals required from client
- Legal/registered company name and approved company description.
- Approved logo source files and brand assets.
- Exact locations and wording for public display.
- Verified contact details and social media links.
- Confirmed operations and crop descriptions.
- Production capacity, acreage, harvest periods and quality standards only if verified and approved.
- Product list, categories, specifications, prices and availability for Agro-Store.
- Approved farm, team, crop and facility photographs.
- Corporate supply terms, minimum order quantities and delivery areas, if applicable.
