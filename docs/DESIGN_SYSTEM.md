# Freshroots Malawi — Web Design System

## Experience principles
1. Minimal corporate presentation.
2. Large cinematic agricultural photography.
3. Strong typography and generous whitespace.
4. Clear paths for corporate procurement and agro-store enquiries.
5. Authentic Malawi identity without clichés or decorative overload.

Use Oasis Group Africa only as a reference for restraint, hierarchy and concise corporate storytelling. Do not reproduce its layout or distinctive elements.

## Layout
- Mobile-first responsive design.
- Use a consistent centred content container with fluid side padding.
- Keep section widths and vertical rhythm consistent.
- Alternate warm cream and white surfaces; reserve deep forest for selected high-impact sections.
- Avoid overusing cards. Prefer editorial two-column layouts, strong image/text pairings and simple grids.

## Colour tokens
- `--color-olive: #687C4C`
- `--color-cream: #F6F1DF`
- `--color-forest: #253C2D`
- `--color-grain: #B69B68`
- `--color-charcoal: #292C27`
- `--color-white: #FFFFFF`

Verify WCAG contrast for actual text/background combinations. Adjust local usage when necessary without arbitrarily changing the brand palette.

## Type
- Headings: Manrope, or suitable fallback.
- Body/UI: Inter, or suitable fallback.
- Use fluid responsive type where useful.
- Keep body text comfortable to read and avoid excessively wide text columns.

## Photography treatment
- Hero should be led by a real Freshroots landscape photograph once supplied.
- Use a subtle overlay only when needed for legibility.
- Use responsive image sizes and modern formats.
- Use meaningful alt text; use empty alt for purely decorative images.
- Do not bake headlines or logos into background photographs.

## Components
Build reusable components for:
- Header and responsive navigation.
- Footer.
- Primary, secondary and text buttons.
- Section heading and eyebrow label.
- Image/text editorial feature.
- Crop/operation feature.
- Product category and product card.
- Enquiry/quotation form fields and validation feedback.
- Call-to-action band.

## Interaction
- Restrained transitions only.
- Keyboard-accessible navigation and controls.
- Visible focus state.
- Clear hover, active, disabled and error states.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- No dead buttons or fake form submissions.

## Homepage section order
1. Header/navigation.
2. Hero with cinematic farm image, short headline and two clear actions.
3. Short company introduction.
4. Operations: rice and maize.
5. Agricultural supplies / Agro-Store introduction.
6. Corporate supply and partnership CTA.
7. Contact CTA and footer.

## Navigation labels
Home, About, Operations, Agro-Store, Corporate Supply, Contact.

## Avoid
- Heavy gradients, glassmorphism, excessive rounded cards, random decorative leaves, cartoon tractors, noisy iconography, autoplay video and excessive parallax.
